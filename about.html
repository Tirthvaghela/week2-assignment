About Larkfield

A small team, on purpose.

We've supported offices across West Yorkshire since 2014. We stay small so the engineer who answers already knows your setup.

Our story

From a spare room to Wharf Street.

Hannah Okafor spent ten years in corporate IT before deciding she would rather know her clients by name. She started Larkfield in 2014 with three local firms and a desk in her spare room.

We have grown slowly since, and deliberately. Twelve engineers is enough to cover holidays and sickness, and few enough that whoever answers has probably visited your office.

  1. 2014

    Founded

    Hannah Okafor left corporate IT and began supporting three local firms from a spare room.

  2. 2017

    First office and service desk

    We moved to Wharf Street and hired a dedicated team to answer calls.

  3. 2020

    Remote work, overnight

    We moved every client to secure home working within two weeks.

  4. 2024

    Security in every contract

    A yearly security review became part of every support plan at no extra cost.
