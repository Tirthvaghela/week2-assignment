Common questions

Before you get in touch.

How long are contracts? +

The first term is 12 months. After that it rolls monthly, and you can leave with 30 days notice.

Do you support Macs? +

Yes. We support Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android, and most offices we look after use a mix.

What are your support hours? +

8am to 6pm, Monday to Friday. Out-of-hours cover for critical systems can be added to any plan.

Do you work outside Leeds? +

We visit offices anywhere in West Yorkshire and provide remote support across the UK.
